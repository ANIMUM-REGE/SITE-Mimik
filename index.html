Common questions

Does my face leave my phone? No. Expression tracking runs on-device with Apple's ARKit. Nothing is uploaded — challenge links carry only which face to make and a score, never any image of you.

What does Mimik Pro include? A one-time $2.99 purchase that unlocks every face in the grid and removes the watermark from shared duet clips. No subscription, nothing recurring.

I bought Pro on another device. Open any locked face and tap “Restore purchase”.

A challenge link didn't open the app. Make sure Mimik is installed, then tap the link again — it opens straight into your friend's challenge.
