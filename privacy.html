😁 Mimik privacy policy

Effective: July 23, 2026

The short version

Mimik reads your face on your iPhone, for the game, and nowhere else. We don't collect your data, we can't see it, and we have no servers to send it to.

Your camera and your face (TrueDepth camera and face data)

Mimik uses the front camera and Apple's ARKit face tracking — which relies on the TrueDepth camera on supported devices — to read your facial expression in real time. All of that processing happens on your device. Your camera feed, your expression data, and your face never leave your phone.

What face data is collected: through ARKit and the TrueDepth API, Mimik reads live blendshape coefficients (numeric values describing your current expression, such as smile, jaw-open, and brow-raise) and the basic face-anchor pose needed to follow that expression. Mimik does not create, store, or export a face model, faceprint, or any biometric template.

How it is used: this face data is used for one purpose only — scoring, frame by frame, how closely your expression matches the target face during gameplay. It is not used for identification, advertising, analytics, or anything else.

Retention and deletion: expression data is processed in memory and discarded frame by frame; it is never persisted. Nothing is retained after the live session.

Sharing: none. Face and expression data are never shared with us or with any third party, and never leave your device. Mimik has no server, no analytics SDK, and no advertising SDK.

The optional clip: during a match, Mimik records a short clip of your attempt so it can build the shareable side-by-side duet. The clip is stored temporarily on your device only, is deleted automatically when you leave the result screen, and is never uploaded by the app. It leaves your phone only if you yourself share or save it from the share sheet.

Challenge links

A challenge link contains only which face to make and the score to beat — for example ?game=mimik&beat=87&seed=3. It never contains any image, video, or personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. Mimik has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party analytics. The app makes no network connections of its own.

Purchases

Mimik Pro is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple's App Store. We receive no personal or payment information from it. You can restore the purchase on any device signed into the same Apple Account.

Photo library

Mimik writes to your photo library only when you tap “Save Video” in the share sheet, and asks for permission the first time.

Children

Mimik collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes & contact
